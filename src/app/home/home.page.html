FAQ

How do I find a room?

Enter a room name or keyword in the search field, then tap Search.

Can I narrow my search?

Use the building and floor filters when they are available to refine your results.

How do I find a room on campus?

Open Map from Quick Access to view the campus map and locate rooms.
